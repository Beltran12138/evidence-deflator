  • Expert Investment Teams — "GPT-4o … with the knowledge cutoff date in August 2023". The provider page says October 2023, which puts September and October 2023 inside the test.「GPT-4o … with the knowledge cutoff date in August 2023」；官方页面写 2023 年 10 月，按官方则 2023 年 9、10 月在测试期内且已读。

The headline model's documented cutoff is 2025-08-31.「Operating in a live-forward setting throughout 2025 … to eliminate look-ahead bias」「T+1 rolling-window backtest using real-time 2025 data」；主模型官方截止日为 2025-08-31。

3 · What the two tables say三 · 两张表说明了什么

  1. None of the nine reaches 48 clean months.9 篇里没有一篇的干净样本达到 48 个月。
  2. Four tested mostly on months the model may already have read.4 篇的测试期大半落在模型可能读过的月份里。
  3. Five of the eight that cannot be assessed used open-weight models that publish no cutoff at all.无从判断的 8 篇里，5 篇用的开源权重模型根本没有公布截止日。
  4. Since 2025 most papers test after the cutoff. The problem is moving from open-book to too short.2025 年以后多数论文已在截止日之后测试，问题正从「开卷」变成「太短」。

Prior work前人工作

This is not a new criterion. The Alpha Illusion lists model version and knowledge cutoff among minimum disclosures; What survives honest evaluation? works the same power arithmetic; Glasserman & Lin (2023) measured look-ahead bias in GPT sentiment signals. What this page adds is narrower: a paper-by-paper table with each cutoff traced to the provider.这不是新判据。Alpha Illusion 把模型版本与知识截止日列为最低披露；What survives honest evaluation? 做的是同一种样本量算术；Glasserman & Lin（2023）最早量化了 GPT 情绪信号的前视偏差。本页的增量更窄：一张逐篇、每个截止日都追溯到模型方的表。

Method and limits做法与限定

  • 105 candidates from five arXiv API queries; 18 selected as LLM trading frameworks reporting historical backtests; 1 excluded (planned experiments, no results). Full texts read 2026-10-05.五组 arXiv 检索得 105 篇候选，筛出 18 篇报告历史回测的 LLM 交易框架，排除 1 篇（只有实验计划）。全文于 2026-10-05 读取。
  • Computed with effective_window from this repository: the cutoff month counts as read; required length for Sharpe 1 at t = 2.用本仓库的 effective_window 计算：截止当月算已读；所需长度按夏普 1、t = 2。
  • "Before the cutoff" means the model may have read it, not that it did.「截止日之前」指无法排除读过，不是一定读过。
  • Headline results only; one auditor; selection may have missed papers.只看主结果；一人审计，候选筛选可能有遗漏。
  • Corrections welcome — and so are additions: three dates and a trial count are enough.欢迎指正，也欢迎补充：三个日期加一个试验次数就够。
