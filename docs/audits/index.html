One page per claim. How much of the evidence counts?

Each audit takes a public AI trading claim and asks for three inputs: the model’s knowledge cutoff, the test window, and how many attempts the result was picked from. Where a project does not publish them, the audit says so.

Rules every audit follows

Sources are public and fetched on the stated date; numbers are recomputed, not copied. “Cannot be ruled out as seen” is the strongest claim made about a model’s training data, never “contaminated”. Findings state what the numbers can and cannot support, with no judgement of intent. Short is not the same as wrong: an underpowered result has not been shown to fail, only not shown to work.
