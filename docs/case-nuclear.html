「今年有 22% 的概率发生核爆」：这个价格作为证据值多少？"A 22% chance of a nuclear detonation this year": what is that price worth as evidence?

2026 年 3 月 4 日，Polymarket 下架「Nuclear weapon detonation by…?」，下架前全年合约报价约 22%。本页只用 Polymarket 公开接口的原始数据和一手来源，对这个数打五道折扣。不评价平台，也不评价下注的人。On 4 March 2026 Polymarket pulled "Nuclear weapon detonation by…?"; the full-year contract was quoting about 22%. This page applies five discounts to that number, using only raw data from Polymarket's public APIs and primary sources. No judgement of the platform or its traders.

1 合约问的，是不是你以为的那个问题？Is the contract asking the question you think it is? 规则原文：任何核爆都算 Yes，「includes offensive usages, nuclear tests, and accidental detonations」。1998 年以后全球每一次核爆都是朝鲜核试验（2006、2009、2013、2016×2、2017），2018 年起为零。另：接口显示三个 2026 合约已于 3 月 11 日按 No 结算，其中两个尚未到期。Rules: any detonation counts, "includes offensive usages, nuclear tests, and accidental detonations". 2 价格能直接读成概率吗？Can the price be read as a probability? 买 No 要付 0.785 并锁到年底（约 0.83 年）。按 4% 资金成本，报价折回约 18.9%。同一平台的研究：10 美分以下的买入平均每美元亏 19.3 美分（低价合约偏贵）；政治类价格长期被压向 50%。Buying No costs 0.785 locked to year-end (~0.83y). At a 4% cost of capital the quote carries back to ~18.9%. Polymarket studies: purchases under 10¢ lose 19.3¢ per dollar (longshots overpriced); political prices compress toward 50%. 方向向下；资金成本只解释约 2.6 个百分点，偏差大小无法精确给出Downward; carry explains only ~2.6pp, the bias size cannot be pinned down band_only
3 这类盘有没有足够的结算记录证明它准？Is there enough settlement history to show it is calibrated? 此前 9 个同类合约（2022-10 → 2025-12）全部 No，日均报价 3%–11%。期限互相重叠，独立观测约 3 年。若真实年概率为 15%，连续 3 年全 No 的机会是 61%；要靠全 No 记录在 5% 水平上推翻 15%，需要 19 年。All 9 earlier contracts (2022-10 → 2025-12) resolved No, average daily quotes 3%–11%. Windows overlap: about 3 independent years. If the true annual rate were 15%, three straight No years happen 61% of the time; rejecting 15% at the 5% level on an all-No record takes 19 years. 「过去都是 No」既不能说明它高估，也不能说明它准"Always No so far" shows neither overpricing nor accuracy underpowered
4 这个价格是多少人定的？How many people set this price? 全年合约公开成交 1,610 笔、约 $80.6k、364 个钱包；前 5 个占 50.4%，前 10 个占 63%。按集中度（HHI = 0.063）折算，相当于约 16 个同等体量的独立钱包。报出 22% 的 3 月 4 日，当天成交约 $4.3k。The full-year contract: 1,610 public trades, ~$80.6k, 364 wallets; top 5 hold 50.4% of notional, top 10 hold 63%. Concentration (HHI = 0.063) is equivalent to about 16 equal-sized independent wallets. On 4 March, the day of the 22% quote, about $4.3k traded. 「市场共识」实际是十几个钱包的共识The "crowd" is a dozen or so wallets thin
5 下注的人能不能左右结果？Can traders influence the outcome? 普通交易者无法引发一次核爆，这一条基本不成立。可能存在的是知情者（例如提前知道试验时间），那会让价格更有信息量，而不是更像激励。No ordinary trader can cause a detonation, so this largely does not apply. Informed insiders (e.g. advance knowledge of a test) are possible; that makes the price more informative, not an incentive. 不打折No discount passes

结论Verdict

22% 是一个约 16 个有效钱包、几千美元日成交给出的「今年会不会有核试验或核爆」的价格。读成概率要向下修，修多少不确定；它和过去 27 年的核试验频率（19%）大致相当。拿它当「核战概率」，题目就错了；拿它当「这个盘准不准」的证据，样本也远远不够。22% is a price on "any test or detonation this year", set by about 16 effective wallets trading a few thousand dollars a day. Read as a probability it should be revised down, by an uncertain amount; it sits close to the 27-year test frequency (19%). As a "probability of nuclear war" it answers the wrong question; as evidence the market is calibrated, the record is far too short.
