FAQ

What is a grade-S Hyperliquid trader?
A grade-S wallet on LiquidWhales is the best of the best — active, low-risk and our top recommendation for live copy. We grade every tracked Hyperliquid wallet with 10+ closing trades S–F from win rate, Sharpe, drawdown, longevity and completed round-trips — all net of trading fees.
Are these grades net of fees?
Yes. Every grade and PnL figure is net of trading fees, so a wallet that looks profitable on gross ROI but bleeds after fees is graded honestly — unlike a raw leaderboard.
