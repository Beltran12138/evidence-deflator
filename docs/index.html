Evidence for trading records

Is that trading record skill, or could it be luck?

Have 47,270 people call ten coin flips each, and about 46 will get all ten right. On a leaderboard, they would be at the top. Leaderboards rank by profit. We rank by how hard a record is to explain by luck. Paste a Hyperliquid address or a Polymarket wallet to check one.

Also on Telegram, @SkillOrLuckBot (send an address, get the verdict), and as a Chrome extension that shows it on Hyperliquid, Binance and OKX copy-trading pages.

Why does no one clear it? Can it ever say yes?

Zero is what a board with no proven skill looks like. If none of the 47,270 accounts had any skill, luck would carry anyone at all past 4.90 only about 2% of the time: that is how the bar is set.

It can say yes; the records are short. The chance that a trader whose true Sharpe is S clears 4.90 after a given length of record:

True Sharpe1 yr2 yr3 yr5 yr
Sharpe 2<1%2%8%33%
Sharpe 33%26%62%96%
Sharpe 418%78%98%>99%
Sharpe 554%99%>99%>99%

The median record in this top 100 is 1.7 years. If every one of these 98 had a true Sharpe of 3, about 22 would clear the bar by now, given their record lengths. 0 do.

How high the bar sits also depends on how independent the accounts are: 4.90 if each is a separate bet, about 2.6 by our rough estimate if many trade alike (3 clear that). Can it say yes · how the bar is set.

Why now

AI makes a good-looking record nearly free to produce. Telling it apart from luck is the scarce part.

SourceAn agent can try fifty variants a minute and keep the best curve. AI trading agents, AI vaults and AI stock tipsters now publish records faster than anyone can check them. This method started on 17 LLM trading papers; none had 48 clean months.
SubjectAI trading competitions, rescored on the same ruler: Alpha Arena, Recall, Liquidity Arena and AI-Trader.
UserAgents that pick a trader or a vault for someone can check the evidence first: an HTTP API and an MCP server, the same numbers as this page.
