Trading journal check

Is your journal's edge real?

Your journal says your best setup is the opening-range breakout, or that Tuesdays are your best day. It found that by comparing every setup, every weekday, every tag. Compare enough slices and one of them will look great by chance. Load the CSV your journal exports and see how much of your record, and of your best slice, luck alone would explain.

The file is read in your browser and never sent to us. Works with exports from Tradervue, TraderSync, TradeZella, Edgewonk, TradesViz or any CSV with a date column and a profit-and-loss column.

How it is computed, and what it cannot see

  • Trades are summed by week (Monday to Sunday, UTC, by the date column) from your first trade's week to your last; weeks with no trades count as 0. Weekly return = that week's P&L over the account size you entered.
  • t is the same statistic as the Hyperliquid wallet check: the weekly mean over its standard error, on the normal scale, with fewer degrees of freedom when the record is short. With no edge, t ≥ 2 turns up about 1 time in 44.
  • The bar rises with how many records you chose from: strategies tried before (your number) times slices compared. Method.
  • It cannot see trades missing from the file, deposits or withdrawals that changed your real account size, or whether fees are in the P&L column.
