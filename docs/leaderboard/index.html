For operators

What would bring the date forward

LONGER WINDOWS

Carry records across phases

A month of live trading is too short for any realistic Sharpe. Carrying Phase I into Phase II doubles each team’s evidence at no cost.

PUBLISH THE HISTORY

Rank only full equity curves

A single snapshot supports no Sharpe, no drawdown and no test. An entry with no history should not sit at the top.

GROUP THE VARIANTS

One owner, one family

Parameter sweeps are one attempt with many tries. Show them as a family against one bar, not as separate winners.
