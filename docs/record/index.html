Calls with a test

1 · Papertrade’s leaderboard does not predict what comes next Pending

Written 2026-10-16, before the freeze (commit 2cd7deb, then the frozen file). Frozen 2026-10-16 03:01 UTC. Readings 2026-10-23 (interim) and 2026-10-30 (primary).

What would count against us: at the 2026-10-30 reading, the 24-hour PnL top 100 beats a seeded random control by more than 15 percentage points in the share of wallets that made money after the freeze, with a median above zero. Then the board carries information, and we will say so. Fewer than 30 wallets trading after the freeze reads as “not enough data”, not as a result. Full text: PREREG.md · audit 010.

2 · Hyperliquid’s top boards hold little forward skill Pending

Frozen 2026-10-14 09:48 UTC: every address on that day’s all-time PnL and 30-day ROI top 100s, 200 wallets, scored only on what they do after the freeze. Reading 2026-12-09, the first day a wallet can have eight weeks of record after the freeze.

Written 2026-10-18. The list and its rule were frozen on 2026-10-14, but the result that would count against us was not written down then; it is written here, four days later and before any wallet can be scored.

What would count against us: of the cohort wallets with at least eight weeks of record after the freeze, more than 10% reach t ≥ 2 on that record at the 2026-12-09 reading. Luck alone gives about 2.3%; if most of them had a true Sharpe of 3, roughly one in five would (normal approximation; the small-sample correction makes it somewhat lower). More than 10% would mean these boards pick up more skill than we say, and we will say so. Reported alongside, with no pass mark: for wallets that were promising before the freeze (t ≥ 2) against the rest, the share with t above zero after it. Fewer than 30 wallets with eight weeks reads as “not enough data”, and the reading moves to 2027-01-06.
